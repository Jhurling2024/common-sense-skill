## Free-first go-to-market sequence

**Stage 0 — publish evidence:** Create public README, MIT LICENSE, CITATION.cff, tests, examples and 2-minute demonstration contrasting a baseline vs this skill. Ensure demonstration does not expose private company data.

**Stage 1 — establish trust:** Publish 10 independently reviewable evaluation scenarios, failure modes, installation instructions, transparent changelog and a no-telemetry statement. Invite community bug reports and contributions.

**Stage 2 — discover demand:** Ask teams whether they want installation help, custom policy packs or workshops. Track actual qualified requests rather than views/stars alone.

**Stage 3 — monetize value:** Enable [GitHub Sponsors](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/setting-up-github-sponsors-for-your-personal-account) after completing eligibility, 2FA, bank and tax information. Offer paid implementation, workshops and support contracts through Marksman Capital USA LLC after ordinary business, tax and IP-authority checks. Make donations optional.

**Stage 4 — scale carefully:** Offer a maintained test harness or hosted reporting product under separate terms (not necessary for core). Keep the free skill complete; make customers pay for integration, service-level commitments or private policy customization—not artificial restrictions on the public skill.
